The ⭐ next to the top songs means on diffrent days each of these songs could possibly be my favourite on this album, they are all so good.

The ❌ next to the bottom songs means it is a song I would instantly skip if it started playing.

Album Review

⭐ ASTROTHUNDER ⭐ This song is amazing. In my top 5 for sure. The way the music flows throuout the song

⭐ STARGAZING ⭐ The beat switch is so good. The flow that comes after the beat switch is so good. i love the drums and lyrical flow in this song too. great rager song too

⭐ SICKO MODE ⭐ Such an amazing song. So many beat switches and flows. i love the beat in this song and the lyrical flow. drake does really good on this song too. everyone knows the lyrics good hype song

BUTTERFLY EFFECT Such a good OG Song. good ragger. love the adlibs in it, easy flow to follow too i love the beat too.

SKELETONS Beutifull song. love the adlibs everything flows so well. The song Travis made when meeting Kylie Jenner. The Weekend on this song is also really good.

STOP TRYING TO BE GOD Such an amazing song. flows perfeclty. atleast top 3 calm travis scott songs.

WHO? WHAT! My favourite rager song on ASTROWORLD, love the flow makes me so hype.

HOUSTONFORNICATION Love the flow so well travis doesnt stop, I love it. one of his best songs where he goes off rapping.

5% TINT Love the beat in this so much its a very recconisable one. flow is so good too. good rager song

NO BYSTANDERS good rager song.

CAN'T SAY Summmer song. Don Toliver does well on this song too.

YOSEMITE Very chill calm song. has good verses.

R.I.P.SCREW This song is nice, the little dings throught the song is nice and has a neat flow with some good verses.

NC-17 The back and fourth beat in this low high pitch that travis does too with his adlibs i like. niche song but not bad.

CAROUSEL Travis verses are good, the feature kinda ruins the song.

❌ COFFEE BEAN ❌ Not my type of song altough i can see how people would enjoy it.

❌ WAKE UP ❌ Terrible song.
