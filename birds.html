Disclamer

The ⭐ next to the top songs means on diffrent days each of these songs could possibly be my favourite on this album, they are all so good.

The ❌ next to the bottom songs means it is a song I would instantly skip if it started playing.

Album Review

⭐ goosebumps ⭐ One of Travis Scott’s best songs. The beat hits hard. Kendrick’s verse is amazing too. super replayable song.

⭐ the ends ⭐ The intro is so good its my favourite part of the song. Flows are so good. Good for late night drives too.

⭐ sweet sweet ⭐ Underrated song. the flow is really smooth and catchy. Good chill song.

pick up the phone (feat. Quavo) Super catchy hook and good energy. Quavo and Travis is a top duo.

sdp interlude Very chill. not much lyrics but i love the girls humming on it, sounds really good. More of a mood song.

outside Solid song. 21 savage is good on this. Beat is nice but not the best.

coordinate Hype rager song. the beat and flow are so good.

through the late night Kid Cudi and Travis combo is really good. Catchy too.

beibs in the trap I dont like Navs voice it irritates me. Travis carries.

wonderful I dont like the Weekend but he does good on this song and sounds smooth. Travis does good too but the feature stands out more.

lose Good song. Doesnt stand out. I like the tone of his voice in this.

first take Bryson Tiller fits well but its kinda slow. Not my favourite.

way back Super mid song just not specail. second half is better than the first. beat switch helps it a lot.

guidance Its a not my type of Travis. Some people would like this I could imagine but not me.
