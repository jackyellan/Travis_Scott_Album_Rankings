Disclamer

The ⭐ next to the top songs means on diffrent days each of these songs could possibly be my favourite on this album, they are all so good.

The ❌ next to the bottom songs means it is a song I would instantly skip if it started playing.

Album Review

⭐ Quintana Pt. 2 ⭐ I love the intro. The energy is insane and Travis goes off. Top 5 Travis songs of all time for me.

⭐ Mamacita (feat. Rich Homie Quan & Young Thug) ⭐ Classic song rager song. This is actually Stormi's favourite song (Travis Scott's daughter). Super catchy. Young Thug and Quan do really good on this.

Drugs You Should Try It One of his best songs. Super chill and emotional. Everything about this song is perfect.

BACC (Bonus) Very aggressive vibe. I really like it, underrated and not talked about enough.

Skyfall (feat. Young Thug) Such a good trap song. Travis and Young Thug flows so well. Young Thug is perfect on this.

Days Before Rodeo: The Prayer Super underrated. The vibe is so good. I love the intro on this song.

Don't Play (feat. The 1975 & Big Sean) Good song. The beat is really nice and catchy. Big Sean is trash on this.

Backyard Chill song. Nothing crazy, but still sounds good. Easy listen.

Sloppy Toppy (feat. Migos & Peewee Longway) Hype song. Good energy but eh, still a good song.

Grey Really chill song. I like this one.

Zombies Good flow and beat. mid song, but still listenable.

Basement Freestyle Ok song. Very repetitive and doesn’t stand out to me. But I woudlent instantly skip this so not terrible.
