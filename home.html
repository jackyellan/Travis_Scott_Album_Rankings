About Travis Scott

Travis Scott, otherwise known as Jacques Bermon Webster II, was born in Houston, Texas, April 30, 1991. When Travis Scott was first coming up, he went by "Travi$ Scott". Travi$ Scott came out with a mixtape called Owl Pharaoh in 2013 followed shortly with the release of Days Before Rodeo in 2014, then Rodeo in 2015 which was a big success. Although Rodeo was the album with the songs that gave him the initial fame, Owl Pharaoh and Days Before Rodeo have songs that are considered some of his best songs to this day. Its just Rodeo is what gave him the first big attention spike from the public. In 2016 is when Travis came out with Birds in the Trap Sing McKnight. This album became his first No. 1 album thanks to the song "Goosebumps" which to this day remains his most played song. After that, Travis dropped ASTROWORLD in 2018 which is seen by a lot of people as his best album. It had huge songs like "SICKO MODE" and really showed how much he improved as an artist. The album was also a tribute to the AstroWorld theme park in Houston that got shut down, which meant a lot to him growing up. Around this time Travis Scott also started dating Kylie Jenner, and they ended up having a kid together named Stormi. This is also relevant to his music because some of his songs were inspired by that time in his life. For example, songs like "SKELETONS" and even later in Utopia "MY EYES" have that more emotional and personal feel compared to his usual hype music. It shows a different side of Travis and how his real life situations can affect his music and what he is releasing. After ASTROWORLD, Travis went quiet for a bit but was still dropping features and staying relevant. In 2023 he finally came back with UTOPIA which had a completely different sound compared to his older music. It was more experimental and darker, but still had some of his best production. Songs like "FE!N" and "MY EYES" show how much he changed his style from albums like Days Before Rodeo, and Rodeo, but still kept what makes him Travis Scott. UTOPIA was one of the most anticipated albums in years and it lived up to the hype for a lot of fans, including me. Travis Scott is known for his unique sound, adlibs, and the way he creates energy and hype vibes in his songs, which is why he is one of the biggest artists in the world today.

Why I Like His Music

For me, Travis Scott has helped me so much in my life. In my day-to-day life, I use music as a tool to make myself calm and relaxed. When I first discovered Travis Scott, he was just a rapper I would listen to. Now I use his music as something that keeps me regulated throughout the day. The thing that his songs have that other artists don’t is his unique autotune voice that is calming to me, and his use of adlibs is my favourite thing about his music by far. The constant and multiple sounds, beats, and lyrics in his music are what make him so different from other artists to me. Travis Scott also has such a diverse portfolio of songs. For example, "Impossible" and "WHO? WHAT!" are both such different songs. One is super calm, slow, and emotional, while the other is a rager, hype song. Even though they are so different, when I listen to both I can relax, listen to the adlibs, and feel familiar with the sound, which makes me feel at peace. Travis Scott, as a person, the energy he represents is so cool to me. He is genuinely the best performer of my generation in my opinion. I also like his clothing and the style he brings outside of music. Overall, his music is something I can always go back to no matter how I’m feeling, and that’s why he is my favourite artist and stands out so much to me compared to other artists.
