Disclamer

This is my favourite album by Travis Scott of all time. When I think about it for a long time, even though its so hard to choose, Rodeo is my top album.

The ⭐ next to the top songs means on diffrent days each of these songs could possibly be my favourite on this album, they are all so good.

The ❌ next to the bottom songs means it is a song I would instantly skip if it started playing.

Album Review

⭐ I Can Tell ⭐ (2:03) That whole minute-long verse is so good. The flow is insane and the beat makes it amazing. One of my favourite songs by him of all time.

⭐ 90210 (feat. Kacy Hill) ⭐ (4:10) Easily one of Travis Scott’s best songs ever. The second half is perfect. The beat, flow, everything is perfect.

⭐ Antidote ⭐ (1:20) Classic Travis song. Everyone knows this song, it was my first every Travis Scott song I heard.

⭐ Nightcrawler (feat. Chief Keef & Swae Lee) ⭐ (4:08) At least top 3 rager songs by Travis. The beat is insane and all the features are perfect on this.

⭐ Never Catch Me ⭐ (1:35) Such an underrated song. The flow is really clean. Travis shows his rapping skills in this well.

Apple Pie (2:01) Amazing outro. The vibe is so nice. One of the best outros.

Impossible (3:45) Very emotional song. The beat is beautiful and Travis sounds great on this.

Pornography (1:31) The beat on this song is so good. I love the autotune on his voice in this song, and his tone switching.

Maria I'm Drunk (feat. Justin Bieber & Young Thug) (0:40) Justin Bieber actually sounds really good here and Young Thug fits perfectly. Classic song.

3500 (feat. 2 Chainz & Future) (0:15) Super hype song. The beat is crazy and Travis goes off. Long but still really good.

Oh My Dis Side (feat. Quavo) (0:42) The beat switch is amazing. Both parts are perfect, but "Oh My" part is my favourite out of the two.

Pray 4 Love (feat. The Weeknd) (2:24) The Weeknd and Travis go well together on this song. Really good song, just not my favourite.

Wasted (feat. Juicy J) (0:03) Good rager. Travis sounds good on this.

Ok Alright (feat. ScHoolboy Q) (5:20) Very underrated, but gets overthrown on this album by all the amazing other songs on it. The second part is super chill.

❌ Piss on Your Grave (feat. Kanye West) ❌ Terrible song. No best part.

❌ Flying High (feat. Toro y Moi) ❌ Terrible song. No best part.
