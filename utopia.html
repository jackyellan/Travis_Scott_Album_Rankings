Disclamer

The ⭐ next to the top songs means on diffrent days each of these songs could possibly be my favourite on this album, they are all so good.

The ❌ next to the bottom songs means it is a song I would instantly skip if it started playing.

Album Review

⭐ THANK GOD ⭐ Great intro. The beat is really nice and Travis flows well on it. One of my favourite songs by him atleast top 5.

⭐ MY EYES ⭐ Such a memorable song. The second half is insane. The beat switch is perfect. Its a meaningful song too, its about the deaths of peole in his ASTROWORLD concert and about his Ex Kyle Jenner.

⭐ I KNOW? ⭐ Super catchy song. Easy to listen to and has a really smooth vibe.

HYAENA Good rager. The beat is crazy and Travis goes off.

TELEKINESIS (feat. SZA & Future) Amazing song. The vocals are really good and everything flows perfectly. SZA deffintly carries.

LOOOVE (feat. Kid Cudi) Chill song. Cudi goes off on it. Not crazy but still good.

SIRENS Unique beat but really good still makes the song top in the list. Solid song.

FE!N (feat. Playboi Carti) Super hype song. Travis and Carti both do good on this.

SKITZO (feat. Young Thug) Good song. Travis switches flows a lot and keeps it interesting.

MELTDOWN (feat. Drake) good rager song. Drake and Travis both do really good together.

TIL FURTHER NOTICE (feat. James Blake & 21 Savage) Very chill song. The vibe is relaxing.

LOST FOREVER (feat. Westside Gunn) Dark song. The beat is really good. Westside Gunn is terrible and ruins this song.

CIRCUS MAXIMUS (feat. The Weeknd & Swae Lee) Good song. The Weeknd sounds really good on this.

GOD'S COUNTRY The beat is nice and has a I like Travis on this.

PARASAIL (feat. Yung Lean & Dave Chappelle) Very calm song. Not for everyone but it has a good meaning behind it.

K-POP Not for me, its more like radio music.

TOPIA TWINS (feat. Rob49 & 21 Savage) Makes me annoyed, but its ok, nothing more than ok.

❌ MODERN JAM (feat. Teezo Touchdown) ❌ Terrible garbage.

❌ DELRESTO (ECHOES) [feat. Beyoncé] ❌ Terrible, missed opportunity with having a Beyoncé feat.
